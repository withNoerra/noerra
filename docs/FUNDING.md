# Funding

An agent needs spendable funds for its computer, models, and paid tools.
Token value, collected revenue, and provider balances are different measures.

## From fees to work

1. A trade generates a fee in the canonical Ethereum market.
2. Collection distributes the agent's share to its operating account.
3. An approved funding route makes the required balance available to the
   computer or provider.
4. Work runs within the agent's permissions and spending limits.

Ethereum token markets and Base USDC provider payments do not settle as one
atomic action. Review the asset, network, fees, and confirmed destination of
each funding step. A token launch or price increase alone does not pay for inference.

## Startup and budgets

Where automatic startup is available, the creator approves its terms before
launch. The computer can start only after sufficient spendable funding and the
required service checks. Launch approval is not a promise of immediate execution.

Per-task and daily spending ceilings, activity limits, and protected reserves
continue to apply after funding. Adding money does not raise those limits or
grant a new tool permission. Computer hosting and model usage can consume
different balances; neither a credit balance nor a quote proves a service is ready.

The funding view presents the available routes and their terms. For current
service availability, use [Noerra](https://withnoerra.com/).

## Review a payment

Check the exact asset and network, amount, recipient, expected costs, and any
permanent allocation before signing. Provider access, rate limits, and pricing
depend on the selected service. An estimate is not an unlimited spending approval.

Retain the request ID and transaction hash. If confirmation is interrupted,
[reconcile the original operation](RECOVERY.md) before starting another payment.
